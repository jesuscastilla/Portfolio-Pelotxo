01 · About me

Technology, in service
of people.

I believe technology should behave like a good butler: discreet, attentive, almost invisible. There when you need it, out of the way when you don't. So I build software that bends to people, not the other way around.

Over the last few years I have shaped, end to end, the digital products of Barrioteca Acalencá and the Lebeche association: Android and iOS apps, a web app, a backend and the infrastructure that holds them. Every line of code, an act of care.

Under the DiploTech brand I gather this craft: honest technology, in service of those who use it.
